Giulio Golinelli

I am a researcher and engineer working at the intersection of applied cryptography, hardware security, and quantitative machine learning. I hold an M.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from the Polytechnic of Milan (2024) and a B.Sc. from the University of Bologna (2021); my Master's thesis on the side-channel analysis of SHA-256 won the national DECIFRIS award. I have worked on post-quantum secure processors, a post-quantum blockchain ledger and quantum key distribution, and real-world hardware side-channel cryptanalysis. Lately I am most interested in distributionally robust deep reinforcement learning for finance — teaching trading agents to plan against the worst-case market. So I have spent time, professionally, on both ends of paranoia. Between Italy, Singapore, and Texas, I have still not found a timezone that agrees with me.
