Giulio Golinelli

I am a researcher and engineer working at the intersection of applied cryptography, hardware security, and quantitative machine learning. I hold an M.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from the Polytechnic of Milan (2024) and a B.Sc. from the University of Bologna (2021); my Master's thesis on the side-channel analysis of SHA-256 won the national DECIFRIS award. I have worked on post-quantum secure processors, a post-quantum blockchain ledger, and real-world hardware side-channel cryptanalysis — so, professionally, I have spent time on both ends of paranoia.

Lately my hobby of choice has been distributionally robust deep reinforcement learning for finance: teaching trading agents to hedge against the worst-case market, which so far makes them more disciplined investors than I am.

I am based in Texas, return to Italy often enough to keep my coffee standards uncompromising, and have also lived in Singapore — so my spice tolerance now comfortably outranks my patience for slow compilers.
